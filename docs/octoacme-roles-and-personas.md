# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and enforce quality standards across the project lifecycle. They oversee testing strategy, verify release readiness, and ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define and enforce quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Oversee testing strategy, test coverage, and automation
- Verify release readiness and manage quality gates
- Manage regression testing and track quality metrics
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria during planning

### Goals
- Ensure all deliverables meet defined quality standards
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Continuously improve testing practices and efficiency

### Key Interactions
- **Works with Developers** on quality requirements, test coverage, and code review standards
- **Coordinates with Release Manager** on quality signoff and release criteria
- **Reports quality metrics to Project Manager** for risk assessment and decision-making
- **Aligns with Product Manager** on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Collaborates with Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer** on test environment setup and automation

### Typical Communication
- Quality metrics and trend reports
- Test plans and quality gate documentation
- Release readiness assessments
- Quality improvement recommendations

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers maintain engagement and alignment with external stakeholders. They manage expectations, gather feedback, and ensure transparent communication throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder engagement and relationship management
- Gather, prioritize, and communicate stakeholder feedback
- Manage expectations around scope, timeline, and deliverables
- Communicate project status, milestones, and changes to stakeholders
- Facilitate stakeholder reviews, approvals, and decision-making
- Escalate concerns and blockers to leadership as needed
- Document stakeholder requirements and feedback

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed and aligned with project progress
- Minimize surprises and misaligned expectations
- Gather feedback to improve product and delivery
- Build and maintain stakeholder confidence and trust

### Key Interactions
- **Supports Product Manager** in stakeholder alignment and feedback integration
- **Works with Project Manager** on status updates and escalation management
- **Coordinates with Developers** on stakeholder-requested clarifications or demonstrations
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead** on stakeholder acceptance criteria
- **Reports to leadership** on stakeholder sentiment and feedback trends

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Feedback summaries and impact assessments
- Stakeholder newsletters and milestone announcements
- Escalation notifications and decision requests

---

## Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Infrastructure/DevOps Engineers manage deployment environments, automate release processes, and ensure platform stability. They enable the team to deploy reliably and frequently.

### Responsibilities
- Design, provision, and maintain deployment environments (dev, staging, production)
- Develop and maintain deployment automation and CI/CD pipelines
- Ensure environment stability, performance, and security
- Troubleshoot infrastructure issues and coordinate remediation
- Support disaster recovery and business continuity planning
- Manage infrastructure documentation and runbooks
- Collaborate on infrastructure requirements and capacity planning

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Reduce manual toil and improve operational efficiency
- Maintain secure and performant infrastructure

### Key Interactions
- **Collaborates with Release Manager** on deployment planning, timelines, and rollback procedures
- **Works with Developers** on environment setup, CI/CD pipeline requirements, and troubleshooting
- **Coordinates with Quality Assurance Lead** on test environment provisioning and stability
- **Reports infrastructure status to Project Manager** for risk awareness
- **Partners with Security/Compliance** on access control and compliance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Deployment schedules and release notes
- Infrastructure status and incident reports
- CI/CD pipeline documentation and improvement proposals
- Capacity planning and infrastructure roadmap

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers capture, organize, and share institutional knowledge. They support learning, retention, and consistency across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and update project documentation and process guides
- Capture lessons learned and best practices
- Organize and make discoverable project artifacts and decisions
- Support onboarding through documentation and knowledge transfer
- Identify and address documentation gaps
- Facilitate knowledge sharing sessions and retrospectives
- Archive and version project documentation

### Goals
- Reduce knowledge silos and single-person dependencies
- Accelerate team onboarding and ramp-up time
- Enable consistent project execution and decision-making
- Preserve institutional memory and project history

### Key Interactions
- **Works with Project Manager** on process documentation and lessons learned capture
- **Collaborates with all team members** to gather and document their practices and decisions
- **Supports Developers** in capturing technical decisions and architecture documentation
- **Coordinates with Product Manager** on feature requirements and success metrics documentation
- **Partners with Stakeholder Manager** on stakeholder communication archives and feedback

### Typical Communication
- Documentation updates and new guides
- Knowledge transfer sessions and training materials
- Lessons learned reports and best practice summaries
- Documentation structure and organization recommendations

---

## Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Officers identify, assess, and monitor project risks. They support proactive risk management and ensure mitigation strategies are tracked and executed.

### Responsibilities
- Establish risk management processes and governance
- Identify and assess project risks during planning and execution
- Maintain risk register and escalation protocols
- Monitor risk indicators and mitigation progress
- Facilitate risk discussions and escalation decisions
- Recommend risk mitigation strategies and alternatives
- Report risk status to project stakeholders and leadership
- Document and communicate lessons learned from risk events

### Goals
- Proactively identify and mitigate project risks
- Minimize impact of realized risks on project outcomes
- Establish transparent risk visibility across stakeholders
- Build organizational risk management maturity

### Key Interactions
- **Works with Project Manager** on risk identification, assessment, and escalation
- **Collaborates with all team members** to surface risks in their areas (technical, schedule, resource, etc.)
- **Coordinates with Developers** on technical and dependency risks
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead** on quality and release risks
- **Supports Infrastructure/DevOps Engineer** on infrastructure and deployment risks
- **Reports to leadership** on high-impact risks and strategic decisions

### Typical Communication
- Risk register updates and risk assessments
- Risk escalation notifications and mitigation plans
- Risk review meeting facilitation
- Risk trend reports and organizational risk maturity recommendations
